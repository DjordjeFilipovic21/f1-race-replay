## Visual Reference Templates (CodePen)

The `templates/` directory contains untracked CodePen projects used as **visual references only** — they are not bundled, compiled, or shipped with the application. These templates remain read-only and are not modified or staged.

### f1-calendar-map

- **Author**: Gabriele Corti (borntofrappe)
- **CodePen**: https://codepen.io/borntofrappe/pen/GRGJYMN
- **License**: MIT
- **Description**: An interactive orthographic globe showing the 2022 F1 calendar with country markers, custom shapes, and chained D3 transitions.

### f1-hero

- **Author**: Sohrab zia (sohrabzia)
- **CodePen**: https://codepen.io/sohrabzia/pen/LEGdMLZ
